### Why this name

`Strain` is the searchable head term and reads as both "a strain" and "strain"
the pressure — which is what the game is about. `Outbreak Lab` carries the
category keywords without stuffing them, and it sets the tone the game actually
has: a laboratory, not a horror film. The pairing is unclaimed enough to rank
for its own name, which is what matters most for a new title.

Alternatives considered and rejected: *Contagion Lab* (crowded head term),
*Spreadpoint* (reads as a spreadsheet tool), *Pathogen X* (the "X" suffix is
noise for ASO).

## Description

### English

```
Strainwave is a strategy puzzle about a pathogen that never existed, in a world
that never was.

You are running a fictional laboratory experiment. Twelve invented territories,
one abstract map, and a strain that starts as a single point on it. Everything
after that is your call: how it travels, how it hides, and what it actually does
to the people it reaches.

A RULE SET YOU CAN READ
The global response is not a black box. It reacts to what it can see — how many
carriers there are and how loud your symptoms are — and it works to a fixed
schedule you can watch and plan against. Once laboratories start work, the game
tells you exactly how many days you have. No dice roll decides your run.

THE TRADE-OFF THAT MATTERS
Every symptom is a bargain. Loud ones spread faster and get you noticed sooner.
Quiet ones buy you time and cost you speed. Transmission gets you everywhere;
resilience keeps you there. There is no build that is simply correct.

WHAT YOU GET
• Thirty abilities across three branches, and a folding mechanic so a wrong turn
  is never permanent
• Five samples, each with a signature behaviour rather than a stat bump
• Three tiers — Breezy, Tense, Lethal
• Two starting worlds: a quiet corner, or a transit hub
• A Daily Challenge with a special condition, identical for every player
• An Outbreak Report after every run: a timeline, a title, and figures worth
  sharing
• Fifteen achievements, from "Never Noticed" to a full ability map

NO INTERNET NEEDED
Plays entirely offline. No account, no login, nothing to sign up for. Your
reports live on your device and nowhere else.

BUILT TO BE READ
Full support for English and Hebrew, light and dark, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver.

Strainwave is fiction. The pathogen, the symptoms, the territories and the
science are all invented, and none of it is modelled on any real disease or
event.
```

## Review notes

```
Strainwave is an abstract strategy puzzle. The pathogen, its symptoms, the
twelve territories and all in-game science are fictional and are not modelled on
any real disease, organisation or event. There is no medical information in the
app, no account system, and no user-generated content.

The game is fully playable offline; no login or demo account is needed. Ads are
served by Google AdMob. Interstitials appear between runs; rewarded videos are
strictly opt-in and only grant in-game evolution points.
```
