Since 28 April 2026, uploads must be built with Xcode 26 or later and the relevant 26-series SDK. Cloud validation uses Xcode 26.3. The app can still support older deployment targets such as iOS 17.

Official source: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications

For this iPhone/iPad app, prepare at least one accepted iPhone screenshot and at least one 13-inch iPad screenshot. Accepted portrait dimensions include 1320×2868 for iPhone and 2064×2752 or 2048×2732 for 13-inch iPad. Native xcresult screenshots are copied without pixel editing and logged in screenshot-evidence.json.
