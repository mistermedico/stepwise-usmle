CardioFocus is an English-language native SwiftUI educational study app for iPhone and iPad. No login, purchases, backend service, tracking, or HealthKit access is required. The curriculum is bundled for offline study.

Suggested review path:
1. Learn: open Hemodynamics & cardiac physiology, then Cardiac output. Mark the unit complete or save it with the bookmark button.
2. Select Practice both questions. Answers receive immediate feedback in untimed practice. Expand option analysis for rationale details.
3. Practice tab: create a timed session. Correct answers are not shown until completion; background time is included in the deadline.
4. Cards tab: tap a card to reveal its concept and mechanism.
5. Progress tab: review local activity, export JSON using the system file picker, or reset local data.
6. About: access references, privacy policy, and support.

There are 240 distinct concepts and 480 paired questions; each pair revisits one concept. Practice accuracy is not a validated exam-readiness measure. The app does not diagnose or treat disease and does not collect health data. Independent medical expert review and full claim-level citation verification are pending; selected claims have been spot-checked against linked professional references. No medical certification or review approval is claimed.

Release should remain manual after Apple approval.
