## Description
```
Yosuf is back - the beloved card game we all grew up with, now polished into an app you can play anywhere.

How to play:
Each player gets 5 cards. The goal: get your hand's value down to 7 points or less, then declare "Yosuf!" But if an opponent is faster and catches you with an even lower hand, they call "Asaf!" - and you pay the price.

What's inside:
• A smart, rule-based opponent engine - from Beginner to Expert, each with distinct play styles
• 5 ready-made rule profiles: Classic, Quick, Grandma's Rules, Street (with surprise event cards!), and fully Custom
• Pass & Play mode for friends sharing one device
• Daily Challenge - the exact same deck and opponents for every player, every day
• Skill rating and progression through named ranks
• "Table Reading" hints based only on what's visible to everyone (toggle on/off anytime)
• Polished design with full Dark Mode, smooth animations, and complete right-to-left Hebrew support
• Classic and Neon card deck skins

Completely free to play, with light, non-intrusive ads that keep development going.

Download now and find out why Yosuf is the card game everyone keeps coming back to.
```
