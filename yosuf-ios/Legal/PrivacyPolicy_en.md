# Privacy Policy for Yosuf: Card Game

**Last updated:** [fill in the date you publish this]

This Privacy Policy describes how Yosuf: Card Game ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App.

## Summary

Yosuf is a single-device card game played against computer opponents. We do not require an account, do not collect gameplay data on a server, and do not have access to your device's contents. The only data collection in the App comes from third-party advertising, described below.

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:
- Your name, email address, or any account information (the App has no account system).
- Your match history, statistics, or in-game achievements — these are stored **only on your device** and are never uploaded anywhere.
- Location data.
- Contacts, photos, or any other personal files.

## Advertising

The App shows ads served by **Google AdMob** to keep the App free. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier) and usage data to show relevant ads, subject to your consent choices below. This data collection is performed by Google, not by us directly.

- **App Tracking Transparency (ATT):** On iOS 14.5+, the App asks for your permission before using data for personalized advertising, in compliance with Apple's requirements. You can decline; the App will still work and will show non-personalized ads instead.
- **Consent (GDPR/EEA/UK users):** The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present a consent form where required by law, letting you choose which types of ad personalization to allow.
- You can review or reset your ad tracking choice at any time in your device's **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

For more detail on how Google AdMob handles data, see [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses data in ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Data Stored On Your Device

The App uses on-device storage (Core Data) to remember:
- Your match history and win/loss record.
- Your skill rating and achievements.
- Your settings (sound, haptics, card skin, hint preferences).

None of this ever leaves your device. Deleting the App deletes this data permanently.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App's advertising is configured to avoid personalized ads for users who are marked as under the age of consent, per Google AdMob's policies.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, by a notice within the App.
